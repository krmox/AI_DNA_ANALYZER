4. KEY FINDINGS AND REMAINING PROBLEMS
--------------------------------------
Findings
- Joint multi-allelic genotyping (A) is the decisive fix. It removes two thirds or
  more of the false positives and also raises recall at both depths. Precision no
  longer falls with depth: 0.941 at 30x and 0.945 at 67x, against the baseline's
  0.833 and 0.768.
- The repeat error model (B) reduces FPs on its own. It gives the best genotype
  concordance, but it barely changes recall and leaves most FPs, because the
  biallelic genotyper still misuses competing-allele reads.
- A+B vs A depends on depth:
    30x  A+B removes 135 more FPs but loses 200 TPs; F1 is lower
         (0.9397 vs 0.9438). The loss is almost entirely in homopolymers
         (recall 0.916 -> 0.878): with the higher estimated slippage rates, a
         heterozygous homopolymer indel at ~15 reads no longer reaches QUAL 20.
    67x  A+B is better than A on F1 (0.9548 vs 0.9516) and on FP (572 vs 644),
         with the same recall.
- Outside repeats, A and A+B lose a little recall vs baseline (0.991 -> 0.989 at
  30x; 0.991 -> 0.988 at 67x), but gain precision (0.967 -> 0.985).
- Cost: A uses ~10% less CPU than baseline (fewer loci than candidates); B adds
  ~16%; peak RSS rises by up to ~15% with A.

Remaining problems (A+B, 30x; hap.py + scripts/analyze.py)
- FN 790:
    158  multi-allelic sites where one allele was never a candidate or is a SNP
         (e.g. chr20:942277 A>AT,T: the T allele is a SNP, which this
         indel-only caller cannot represent)
    120  a different representation was called (e.g. truth AC>A + AAC>A 4 bp
         apart, called as one AAAC>A; compound haplotypes are not modelled)
    116  never a candidate (discovery)
     95  wrong genotype
     17  filtered
- FP 539: 405 wrong-length or representation calls next to a real variant
  (252 in HP>=7), 92 genotype errors, 42 with no true variant nearby.
- Clustered variants (another truth variant within 10 bp): recall 0.786 vs
  0.965 isolated.
- Homopolymer recall at 30x with B: 0.878. The slippage model is right on
  average, but the fixed QUAL >= 20 is too strict for low-depth homopolymer
  hets. That threshold was not tuned here and must not be tuned on this set.
- Wall-time measurements are noisy on this machine (see section 2).

5. VERDICT
----------
A    IMPROVED at 30x and 67x: F1 +0.070 / +0.113, FP -67% / -79%, FN -33% / -45%,
     CPU -8% / -10%. Genotype concordance slightly lower (0.988 -> 0.986 at 30x;
     0.993 -> 0.987 at 67x), because many more difficult loci are now matched.
B    IMPROVED at 30x and 67x on F1 (+0.013 / +0.031), FP (-22% / -28%) and
     genotype concordance (0.993 / 0.995). DEGRADED recall at 30x (-0.009,
     +94 FN); at 67x recall is unchanged (+5 FN). Costs +16-17% CPU plus a
     7-11 s estimation pass.
A+B  IMPROVED vs baseline at both depths (F1 +0.065 / +0.116). Relative to A
     alone it is MIXED: worse at 30x (F1 -0.004, recall -0.018 in total and
     -0.038 in homopolymers), better at 67x (F1 +0.003, FP -11%).

This experiment is development data. The independent validation on HG001 chr19 30x
(EXPERIMENTS.md) gave A F1 0.9609 and A+B 0.9637. No confidence intervals have been
computed, so the A vs A+B ordering is not established.
