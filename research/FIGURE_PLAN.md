source or read from the cached array; nothing is simulated. Figures 1–7 were generated on 2026-09-19 and
visually inspected for layout (all seven opened; layout defects in Figures 1, 2, 3 and 5 were corrected and
re-generated; Figures 1 and 5 were regenerated after the last edit and not re-opened — **final review by the author is still required**).

## Status after the 2026-09-20 revision (chr20 and `load_reads` added to the manuscript)
No figure was regenerated. Figures 1–7 predate the chr20 run and the `load_reads` optimisation. Consequences, stated in the manuscript legends: Fig. 2 and Fig. 3 do not show v20 (its ΔF1 −5.64×10⁻⁵, CI [−1.05×10⁻⁴, −1.4×10⁻⁵], lies inside the grey ±0.001 band) or the third hap.py point (−5.5×10⁻⁵); Fig. 4(a) shows 8 of the 9 pre-chr20 lost SNPs (the tenth, chr20:46,110,517, is in Table 4); Fig. 6 shows only the historical counts-only configuration (1,392 s → 1,140 s), and the current profile (native counts + reads; PB 93–96 %) is given in text/Tables 5d–5e. The chr20 and performance results are presented as Tables 2, 3b, 3c, 5d, 5e. Regenerating figures is optional and was deliberately not done in the freeze phase.

## Considered and not made
* **Forest of hap.py CIs**: no bootstrap was computed for the hap.py results, so no plot would be honest.
* **End-to-end cost vs. depth or genome size**: no measurements beyond 3 Mb (pipeline) / 12 Mb (extraction only) exist.
* **Head-to-head runtime plot**: no like-for-like runtime for the AI pipeline exists at the external-caller scope.
* **Genotype confusion matrices**: numeric tables (Table 5a) are sufficient; matrices are in `experimental/genotype_layer/RESULTS_VALIDATION*.md`.
* **Threshold-selection curve** (validation F1 vs router coverage): the values live only in the router-freeze worktree's devlog 13 §7 and were not machine-readable here; a figure would need re-extraction. `[DATA VERIFICATION NEEDED]`
